## Пример

**Задача `math-train/324`.** Упростить $\sqrt{6+4\sqrt2}+\sqrt{6-4\sqrt2}$.

```text
<think>
<Parallel>
<Goal>
<Outline>1: Simplify the positive-radical term.</Outline>
<Outline>2: Simplify the negative-radical term using its positive square root.</Outline>
</Goal>
<Path>
1: The radicands can be written as squares.

6 + 4sqrt(2) = (2 + sqrt(2))², whose positive square root is 2 + sqrt(2).
</Path>
<Path>
2: 6 - 4sqrt(2) = (2 - sqrt(2))². Because 2 > sqrt(2), its positive square root is 2 - sqrt(2).
</Path>
<Conclusion>
Add the two roots.
</Conclusion>
</Parallel>

Adding the two roots cancels the radical terms.
</think>
Final Answer: 4
```
