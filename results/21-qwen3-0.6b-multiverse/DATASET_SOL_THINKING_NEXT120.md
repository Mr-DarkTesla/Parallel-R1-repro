## Пример

`math-train/6553`: две стороны прямоугольного треугольника равны 4 и 5; третья сторона зависит от роли пятёрки.

```text
<think>
<Parallel>
<Goal>
<Outline>1: Find the missing side when the longer given side is the hypotenuse.</Outline>
<Outline>2: Find the missing side when both given sides are legs.</Outline>
</Goal>
<Path>
1: If the side of length 5 is the hypotenuse, the two legs have lengths 4 and sqrt(5²-4²) = 3. Thus the missing side can be 3.
</Path>
<Path>
2: If 4 and 5 are both legs, the Pythagorean theorem instead makes the missing hypotenuse sqrt(4²+5²) = sqrt(41). Length 4 cannot be the hypotenuse because another given side is 5.
</Path>
<Conclusion>
Multiply the possible missing side lengths and round.
</Conclusion>
</Parallel>

The product of possible lengths is 3sqrt(41) = 19.209..., which rounds to 19.2.
</think>
Final Answer: 19.2
```
